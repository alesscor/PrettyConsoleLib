# Code of Conduct

We want an open, welcoming, and productive community. All contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to help create a respectful environment.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (issues, PRs, mailing lists, chat, and events) and to all interactions related to this repository.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and considerate in language and actions.
- Assume good intent and provide constructive, actionable feedback.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.
- Follow repository guidelines (`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `.editorconfig`) and keep discussions on-topic.

## Unacceptable Behavior
The following are not tolerated:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Threats, doxxing, or publishing private information.
- Sustained disruption of project processes or abusive behavior toward contributors.
- Posting sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise offensive material.

## Reporting & Enforcement
If you experience or witness a violation, please:
1. Try to resolve it politely and privately if appropriate.
2. Open an issue titled `CODE OF CONDUCT: <short description>` and clearly describe the incident, or contact a maintainer (for this project: @alesscor) with details.

Maintainers will review reports, may contact involved parties for clarification, and may take actions such as removing content, asking for change, temporarily or permanently banning contributors, or escalating to hosting platform channels as needed.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct follows common community guidelines and is intended to be short, clear, and practical. It may be updated by maintainers; changes will be documented in the repository.
